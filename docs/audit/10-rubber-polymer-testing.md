## Rubber and Polymer Testing conducted at Auto Cluster (verbatim)

**H2:** Rubber and Polymer Testing conducted at Auto Cluster

> Rubber and Polymer testing is a trusted methodology that determines the physical properties of the material when exposed to various conditions like Heat, Fuel and Oil. These tests are used to determine the physical and mechanical properties of raw materials and components. At Auto Cluster we are equipped to undertake below mentioned tests.

Fourteen accordion items follow. Each marked up as `<h1>` prefixed with `>`, linked to an empty `<>` target. 🔴

### 1. Thermal Testing
> Auto Cluster has a well-equipped Thermal Testing Lab in Pune, and we help our clients in validating various physical properties of their products. Our collection of Machines for Thermal Testing are Ovens, Hardness Tester, Tensile and Density.
> Thermal testing is used for checking changes in various physical properties in the Rubber Industry.

### 2. FTIR Testing
> FTIR testing is used to identify various organic and inorganic materials in a substance. At our FTIR Testing Lab in Pune, we help in the identification of Polymer and finding out unknown material across the various products used in Rubber and Polymer Industry. We have the latest machinery required for FTIR testing.
> FTIR Testing is used across Rubber and Polymer Industry.

### 3. TGA Testing
> TGA Testing is also known as Thermogravimetric analysis and is a method of thermal analysis that measures the product/sample in temperature change. At our TGA testing Lab in Pune, identification of the composition of the material is done. With the testing checks on the Polymer content, filler content and ash content is done for material used across Rubber and Polymer industry. We have the latest TGA machinery for various testing.
> TGA Testing is used in Rubber and Polymer industry.

### 4. DSC Testing
> DSC Testing or Differential scanning calorimetry testing is used to identify how the properties of a material change along with the temperature against time. At our DSC Testing Lab in Pune, we help in determining the melting point, crystallization and degradation of the various products being used by our clients. We are equipped with appropriate DSC Testing Machinery.
> DSC Testing is used in Rubber and Polymer industry.

### 5. Xenon Weathering Testing
> Xenon Weathering Testing is used to check the weathering effect on a sample in light, moisture, heat and water spray. At our Xenon Weathering testing lab in Pune, we work with our clients and help them in analysing various weathering effects. We have Xenon Test Chamber that meets all the required national and international standards.
> Xenon Weathering testing is used by numerous industries like Rubber, Polymer, paint, cosmetics, adhesives, sealants, printing-ink/artist material/paper, packaging, textile, and pharmaceuticals.

### 6. Fatigue Testing
> Fatigue Test is used to check the life of a component and strength o the material. At our Fatigue Testing Lab in Pune, we work with our clients from Rubber, Polymer and Metal industry to check the product's performance under application condition. Intron Actuator is the machine used for Fatigue Testing.
> Fatigue testing is used in Rubber, Polymer and Metal industries.

⚠️ "strength o the material" — missing "f". "Intron" → **Instron** (the machine card spells it correctly).

### 7. Ozone Testing
> Ozone Testing is done to check the zone effect on the given sample in an ozone atmosphere. At our Ozone Testing Lab, we help to determine various cracking criteria for our clients. We have an Ozone Chamber for conducting the necessary Ozone Testing.
> Ozone testing is used in Rubber and Polymer industries.

⚠️ "the zone effect" → "the ozone effect".

### 8. Weathering Testing
> Weathering testing is used to check the weathering effect on a sample in UV light, Condensation, heat and water spray. At our Weathering Testing Lab in Pune, we analyse the various weathering effects. Auto Cluster has UV Whethr-O-meter machine for conducting these tests.
> Weathering testing is used across industries of Rubber, Polymer, paint, cosmetics, adhesive, sealants, printing-ink/artist material/paper, packaging, textile, and pharmaceuticals.

⚠️ "Whethr-O-meter" → **Weather-Ometer** (Atlas trademark).

### 9. Tensile Testing
> Tensile Testing is used to check stress v/s strain properties of a sample. At the Tensile testing lab in Pune, the determination of various mechanical properties of the materials is done. Auto Cluster has the latest Universal testing Machine (UTM) for conducting Tensile Testing.
> Tensile Testing is used in Rubber and Polymer industry for checking samples.

### 10. Flexural Testing
> Flexural Testing is used to measure flexural strength and flexural modulus of a sample. At the Flexural Testing Lab in Pune, we measure the force required to bend a beam of plastic material and determines the resistance to flexing or stiffness of material for our client from the Polymer industry. Auto Cluster has the latest Universal testing Machine (UTM) for conducting Flexural Testing.

### 11. Endurance Testing
> Endurance Testing is used to check the life of a component. At the endurance testing lab in Pune, measuring the force required to bend a beam of plastic material and determining the resistance to flexing or stiffness of a material is done for our clients in Rubber, Polymer and Metal industry. Auto Cluster has the latest Intron Actuator for conducting Flexural Testing.
> Endurance Testing is used in Rubber, Polymer and Metal industries.

🔴 This entry describes **flexural testing**, not endurance testing — the body text was copy-pasted from item 10 and not rewritten. It also ends by saying the Instron Actuator is used "for conducting Flexural Testing". Two errors in one item. "Intron" again.

### 12. Heat Ageing Testing
> Heat aging testing is to determine the Physical properties under the effect of Heat, Fuel and Oil of a sample. The Heat aging testing lab in Pune determines and checks the change in the physical properties of the sample for the Rubber Industry. Auto Cluster has the latest Ovens, Hardness tester, tensile and density tester for heat ageing testing.
> Heat Ageing Testing is used in Rubber industries.

### 13. Oil Ageing Testing
> The Oil aging testing is to determine the Physical properties under effect of Heat, Fuel and Oil of a sample. The Oil Aging Testing lab in Pune determines and checks the change in physical properties of the sample for the Rubber Industry. Auto Cluster has the latest Ovens, Hardness tester, tensile and density tester for oil ageing testing.
> Oil Ageing Testing is used in Rubber industries.

⚠️ Items 12 and 13 are near-identical; both claim to test "under the effect of Heat, Fuel and Oil".

### 14. Material Testing
> The Material Testing is used to find out Unknown material in a sample. At the material testing lab in Pune, checks in polymer identification is done for Rubber and Polymer industries. Auto Cluster has the latest FTIR machine for Material testing.
> Material Testing is used in Rubber and Polymer industries.

### 15. Mechanical Testing
> Mechanical Testing is used to check stress v/s strain properties. At our Mechanical Testing Centre in Pune, we determine the mechanical properties of the materials for rubber and polymer industries. Auto Cluster has the latest Universal Testing Machine (UTM) for Mechanical Testing.
> Mechanical Testing is used in Rubber and Polymer industries.

⚠️ Items 9 and 15 both describe "stress v/s strain properties" using the UTM — substantially duplicate.
⚠️ "Ageing"/"aging" mixed within the same items.
⚠️ **No test standards cited** in any of the 15 items — even though the machine modals *do* carry them (ASTM D412, ISO 178, ASTM D1238, ASTM E1252, ISO 180/179, ASTM D1171/D1149). The standards exist in the system; they are simply not shown where buyers look.
