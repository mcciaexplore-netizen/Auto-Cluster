## About Our Environmental Testing Facility (verbatim)

**H2:** About Our Environmental Testing Facility

> Environmental testing is the measurement of the performance of equipment under specified environmental conditions, such as extremely High and Low Temperatures, blown and settling Sand and Dust, Salt spray and Salt fog, Very high or Low Humidity, and many others. Such tests are most commonly performed on Aeronautical, Space applications, and in Automotive.

> In Environmental testing, most of the parts, components, sub-assemblies are validated by using National and International Test Standards. An Environmental chamber also called a Climatic chamber is often used for these tests. Chamber testing involves testing and exposing products to various environmental conditions in a controlled setting environment. At Auto Cluster, we serve 200+ valuable and satisfied (MSMEs, OEM and MNC) customers in the environmental testing areas. We are also equipped to undertake special projects like Water Drop Test for our clients.

### Content problems across these 11 items

🔴 **"birthing action"** appears four times (items 1, 2, 3, 10) — this is almost certainly a corruption of **"breathing action"**, a standard term in temperature-humidity cycling. Reproduced verbatim here, but it must be corrected.
🔴 **"electronica"** appears three times (items 4, 5, 11) — should be "electronic".
🔴 **"the atom shall examine"** / **"the atoms shall examine"** / **"the atom is examined"** (items 4, 5, 11) — "atom" appears to be a corruption of "item" or "unit". Nonsensical as written.
⚠️ Items 1, 2, 3 and 10 are ~90% identical text. Items 4 and 11 (Water Spray / Rain) are near-duplicates, as are the descriptions of the same chambers.
⚠️ "Ageing" in the heading, "Aging" in the body (item 8).
⚠️ Stray spaces before commas and full stops in items 4 and 9.
⚠️ Item 10 ends with no full stop.
⚠️ **No test standards cited anywhere** — no ASTM B117 for salt spray, no IEC 60529 / IP ratings for water and dust ingress, no ASTM D1171. For a NABL lab this is the most important missing information on the page.
